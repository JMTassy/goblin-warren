## 1. The inversion

For twenty-five years agencies sold **confidence** — the beautiful deck that
made the client feel the bet was safe. Generative AI just made confidence free:
anyone can produce a flawless-looking, fully confident artifact in an afternoon.
When confidence is free, it stops being the product.

What cannot be generated is **calibration** — the difference between what a
document *knows* and what it merely *says*. Calibration is scarce, expensive to
produce honestly, and impossible to counterfeit *in the presence of receipts*.

> The governed agency does not sell confidence. It sells calibrated confidence,
> and prices the calibration.

The mechanism is not new to the house. Case studies already close on
« filmé par… », « illustrations de… ». The governed deliverable extends that
exact gesture one layer down: **the receipt is the craftsman credit for a
claim.** Naming the source of a number is the same act of craft-respect as
naming the photographer. This is not a new value. It is the old value applied
at claim-grain.

## 2. The guarantee (the one-sentence contract)

Every governed deliverable carries, signed, on the cover:

> « Aucun chiffre de ce document n'a été produit sans source. Tout ce qui n'est
> pas sourcé est marqué "à chiffrer". Ce que nous avons écarté est en annexe,
> avec les raisons. »

That sentence is the moat. It is cheap to print and ruinous to fake: one
audited counterexample destroys the house that signs it falsely — which is
precisely why signing it truthfully is worth money.

## 4. Product architecture — three tiers, laboratory-ordered

**T1 — LE FORMAT (the Governed Deliverable).** Any strategy or creative
document restructured so the hero layer stays pure — render-led, no bullets,
image-first — and a **ledger annex** carries the epistemics: a posture on every
claim, a receipt per claim, the compost annex (what was refused, with reasons),
the fog annex (what is held open), and the falsifiable box (the central bet and
what would disprove it). Normative definition in `SPEC.md`; skeleton in
`TEMPLATE.md`.

**T2 — L'AUDIT (the Gate Audit).** Entry product, BAAS-speed. Take a client's
*existing* AI-generated artifact and run it through the gate. Deliver the
verdict table: what survives with receipts, what composts with reasons, what
fogs. Cheap, self-demonstrating, and it sells T1 automatically, because every
audit ends with the client looking at their own unsourced numbers.
Delivery window, price point, and effort: **à chiffrer**.

**T3 — LE LABORATOIRE (pilot on owned ground).** The house's oldest move
pointed at the new capability: run a governed-AI activation on owned property
at own risk, produce the honest bilan as the public case study, then sell the
proven format — the franchising instinct, with the gate as the thing
franchised.

Order matters: **T2 funds and evangelises, T1 is the core, T3 is the proof
engine.** Sequencing rationale: `PROPOSED`. Revenue and margin per tier:
**à chiffrer**.

## 5. The objection, met head-on

*"Receipts will read as self-doubt."*

Two answers, one linguistic and one empirical.

**Linguistic.** `constaté` is not a hedge, it is a flex. The posture vocabulary
in `SPEC.md` is tuned so that a sourced claim reads as strength and an unsourced
one as honest humility — the register of a terroir label, not of a disclaimer.

**Empirical, and it can fire.** `AB_FALSIFIER_PROTOCOL.md` specifies one real
pitch produced in two versions, clean and governed, shown to real decision-
makers. If the clean version consistently wins and the receipts read as
hedging, **the idea dies and we say so, in the ledger.** The protocol fixes its
thresholds before the test runs, so the result cannot be renegotiated after it.
