# HELD — AURA fog registry

Registry of held proposals — the AURA fog. A proposal the verifier marks HOLD
enters here instead of being denied or admitted. Held proposals are revisited
deliberately on a weather pass (loop step 7): each is re-proposed, denied, or
left in fog with a fresh timestamp. Fog is never silently dropped and never
silently admitted. A thick registry is the uncertainty metric — the signal to
schedule a weather pass.

Format: `DATE · description · reason held`
