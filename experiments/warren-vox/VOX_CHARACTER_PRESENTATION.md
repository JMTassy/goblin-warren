# VOX_CHARACTER_PRESENTATION.md — how goblins present under WARREN VOX

<!-- WARREN VOX · authority=false · claim=NO_CLAIM · non-sovereign · cost:0 · render-layer only -->

Companions in the Warren are **expression, never authority**. This document
says how Bram — and later Lulu and the specialization roster — look, portrait,
speak, feel, and hint, and it fences all of it inside one boundary: **companion
presentation is a read-only projection of state that can propose but never
mutate.** VOX renders a goblin; the engine still decides every consequence.

Naming (per the assets, do not conflate):
- **Bram** — the opening-loop companion of the current lineage
  (`SPRITE_SPEC.md`, Levels 0–6, trace bias `warning`). The canonical sprite.
- **Lulu** — the **Level-7 second companion** (adoption spine, trace biases
  `relational`, `novel`). Not a rename of Bram; a distinct later character.
- Roster (Levels 4–7, `TRACE_SYSTEM.md`): **Forgeron** (`warning`), **Moss** /
  Collecteur (`resource`), **Keeper** / Chaman (`warning`, `memory`).

## The boundary (cite it before anything else)

The vertical slice states the law in its core header and enforces it:

> *"Companion speech is expression-only: `proposeCompanionLine(S)` derives a
> line; no action kind carries free text into δ, so a generative layer plugged
> into the companion channel can propose but can never mutate world, quest, or
> reward state."*  — `slice-core.js`

`proposeCompanionLine(S)` returns `{speech, emotion, provenance}` from a curated
table keyed on phase/level; `hintFor(S, id)` returns `{guess, confidence,
provenance:'companion_signal'}`. **Neither is on any `applyEvent` path.** The UI
renders them and nothing else — there is no text-input route into the reducer.
VOX generalizes this: *any* companion channel (curated table, a future local
model, a memory-derived recall) enters through the same door — it produces a
line + a provenance tag for VOX to render, and it reaches `applyEvent` **never**.

A VOX character component therefore has this exact signature and no other power:

```
renderCompanion(projection.companion) → DOM   // {speech, emotion, provenance}
```

It takes the proposed line. It draws a bubble with a visible provenance label.
It cannot admit, reward, unlock, or persist anything.

## Sprite presentation

Governed entirely by `warren-design/SPRITE_SPEC.md` — VOX does not restyle the
sprite, it *presents* it:

- **Canonical form**: canvas-drawn nearest-neighbor pixel sprite, fixed 10×10
  buffer box, `fillRect` blocks, palette exclusively from `tokens.css`. No
  emoji, no DOM sprite, no image asset, no CDN. (VOX may never swap this for a
  web-font glyph — that would break the palette lock and the single-resolution
  law.)
- **Warming**: body/belly/ears mix cold→warm on the *same* `grade` as the room
  (`--moss`/`--moss-warm`, `--belly`/`--belly-warm`, `--ear`/`--ear-warm`),
  gaining a `--rim` fire rim-light and `--glow`→`--glow-hot` eye-glow as the
  scene lights. Synchrony is the point: she belongs to the world.
- **Poses** are *selected*, never authored, by existing engine signals — the
  five named states from the spec: `idle`, `shiver`, `curious`, `warm-hands`,
  `content`. VOX's only job is to pick the right delta from the projection
  (`fire.state`, `grade`, `S.mark`, a shake timer), draw it, and — under reduced
  motion — drop the shiver jitter (already the spec's rule).
- **New characters** (Lulu, Forgeron, Moss, Keeper) must each ship a
  `SPRITE_SPEC.md`-shaped contract — same 10×10 bounding-box convention, same
  `tokens.css` palette — before they earn the character layer. VOX will not
  render a goblin that lacks a sprite contract; it will not improvise one.

### Portrait presentation

A **portrait** is a framed, larger crop of the same buffer sprite (never a new
illustration) shown beside the speech bubble in gates/dialogue-heavy moments. It
is a *camera framing* of the character layer — same pixels, same palette, scaled
on the `PIX` lattice with `image-rendering: pixelated`. VOX may add a chunky
token-bordered frame (`--bw` + `--vox-shadow-3`, no radius/blur) but may not
repaint the face.

## Speech bubble + PROVENANCE LABEL (mandatory)

Every rendered companion line carries a **visible provenance label** — this is
non-negotiable and is the whole reason the provenance band exists. VOX renders
one of four labels, driven only by the tag the channel returned:

| Label | Meaning | Source tag (example) |
|---|---|---|
| **curated** | hand-written line, fixed | `curated` / `curated_fallback` |
| **model** | generated by a local model at expression time | `model` (future gateway) |
| **memory_derived** | recalled/derived from the ledger trace | `memory_derived` (replay recall) |
| **signal — not proof** | an honest but fallible read (a hum/guess) | `companion_signal` |

The slice already renders three of these live: the say-bubble prints
`curated · <emotion>` or `signal · <emotion>`; the hum-bubble prints the fixed
`bram's hum · signal — not proof`; the free-lesson override prints `curated ·
free lesson`. VOX's rule makes the pattern total:

- **No line renders without a label.** A bubble with an empty/absent provenance
  is a VOX conformance failure (checked in `VOX_VISUAL_TESTS.md`).
- The label uses the quiet micro-label register — `--step-0`,
  `--tracking-label`, uppercase, `--dim` — under the line, so it reads as a
  receipt, not a shout, but is always present and legible.
- **`signal — not proof` is load-bearing.** A hum is Bram's honest guess
  (`hintFor` is right ~8/10, sometimes confidently wrong). VOX must present it
  as a *signal the player may choose to verify*, never as truth. The listening
  lens (VERIFY) is what turns signal into proof; the bubble must never dress a
  hum as certainty.

Bubble styling follows HUD law: `--panel` fill, `--bw` + `--shadow-1` border,
no radius/blur, `--step-1` body in `--glow` (affirming voice) or `--bone`,
opacity fade over `--vox-dur-bubble`.

## Emotion states

`proposeCompanionLine` returns an `emotion` (`cold_earnest`, `attentive_calm`,
`warm`, …). VOX maps emotion to **presentation deltas only** — never to new
powers:

- **Sprite**: emotion nudges pose selection within the five spec states (warm →
  `content` bob; attentive → `idle` steady; a disappointed beat → the shake
  timer). No new geometry.
- **Bubble**: emotion tints the voice within the allowed palette (affirming
  `--glow` vs neutral `--bone`) and sets the fade envelope; it never changes the
  border/shadow idiom.
- **Label unaffected**: emotion is expression; provenance is source. They are
  orthogonal and both shown (`curated · warm`).

Emotion is a *view* of state; VOX must not persist a mood or let it accumulate
across sessions (ZOL/session-only law).

## The hummed-hint presentation

The hum is the game's cleanest teaching object and gets a dedicated treatment:

- Rendered in its **own** bubble (`#hint`), positioned near the thing it's about,
  in `--bone` with the guessed verdict word in `--glow` (`.guess`).
- Always labelled `bram's hum · signal — not proof`.
- Sentence comes from `hintFor` via a fixed phrasing table (`hintSentence`) —
  curated strings selected by `{guess, confidence}`, never free-generated at the
  render layer.
- Presented as *invitation to verify*, not instruction: the lens/VERIFY path is
  always available beside it. VOX visually couples hum and lens (e.g. the lens
  cursor active while a hum shows) so the player learns the move: *hear the
  signal, then check it.*
- The hum reads state and proposes; catching/verifying still routes through
  `applyEvent`. The hum changes nothing on its own.

## The absolute non-goal

Nothing in this document — no pose, no emotion tint, no bubble, no portrait, no
hum — may mutate progression, inventory, receipts, ledger state, or a witness
gate. If a future companion is powered by a local model, that model plugs into
the **propose** side of `proposeCompanionLine`/`hintFor` and inherits this exact
fence: **it may say anything and change nothing.** The deterministic engine
gates every consequence.
