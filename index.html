🤔 When a goblin has an idea

A goblin may bring you a proposal. Three buttons appear:

TRY IT — let it happen · HOLD IT — wait and think · COMPOST IT — say no, and the idea rots into rich soil for later. Nothing changes the Warren unless you tap.

🏆 What you're growing

Buy and evolve territories, teach your goblins, and keep Lulu happy. The Warren is a tiny place that notices you — the more you tend it, the more it becomes yours.

🔣 The Warren's signs

Every sign here means one thing, always — a word, not a decoration.

Your mark: 🌱 accepted · ⏳ held · 🍂 returned to soil. The same three whether you stamp a goblin's idea or the day's verdict.

A goblin's feeling (the sign floating above them): ✨ curious · 🌟 happy · 〜 calm · ♡ lonely · ◆ focused · ❣ dramatic.

What stirs (in the inspector): 🐛 a bug · 😴 tiredness · ✨ a mystery · 🌫️ intrusion · 🧭 a new path · 🌳 the Tree's wonder.

The places: 🌳 Tree · 🌸 Garden · 🪲 Nursery · 🏰 Spire · 🔥 Forge · 🍄 Gate.
