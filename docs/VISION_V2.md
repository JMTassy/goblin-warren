## 0 · The introspection — what the record actually shows

Every fact below is checkable against the git log or a witness quote.

**Three witnessed lessons built this game. Nothing else did.**

1. *"Everything comes at once… the magic is not happening"* (witness 1,
   2026-07-14) → the Bonding Ladder: gate the machine behind one goblin.
2. *4/10 — "not alive"* (witness 2, same day) → make Lulu a **being**:
   her own dusk world, sleep/stir/wake, gesture-read, her real voice.
3. *"I first love it, then get bored because I do not see the step by step
   progression, so I leave"* (witness 3, 2026-07-15) → the mystery-tap fix
   + visible progress dots + Rung 4's real scarcity.

Each lesson cost the operator ~90 seconds of real play and reshaped the
game more than any document in `docs/`. **The witness is the only
instrument that has ever measured fun.**

**One failure pattern nearly drowned it.** Between witnesses, design
generation ran nearly free: at least six complete, mutually incompatible
campaign structures arrived (Level 1–12 reframes, chaos-magic egregore
arcs, stigmergy systems, collective-intelligence MVPs, daily-core-loops
L1/L2) — plus three *independent* critique passes that all converged on
the same verdict: **stop drafting campaigns, build one real loop.** The
drafts kept arriving anyway. The ratio at introspection time: **42
documents, 4 built rungs.** Design costs nothing; witness costs a real
thumb; an ungated flood of the free thing starves the expensive thing.

**And the false-green pattern is alive right now.** The v5 deploy
returned `success:true` while the live URL serves a bare `ok` placeholder
(operator screenshot, 2026-07-16 13:33). Same law as the pipeline exit
codes and the wolf-math error before it: **the measurement path is part
of the certifier.** A deploy is not done when the API says so; it is done
when the operator's browser shows the Warren.

**The convergence is the signal worth keeping.** Strip the six competing
drafts to what they *independently agree on* and one mechanical core
remains — the same core Rung 4 just implemented in miniature. That core
is V2.

V1's implicit question was **"how much game can we build?"** — it
produced 42 documents.

V2's question is **"what does the next thumb-touch prove?"** — it
produces witnessed slices.

The Warren's own deepest law now applies to its makers:
`memory = function(event_log)` → **vision = function(witnesses)**.

## 2 · The game, one paragraph (V2 core fantasy)

A tiny living warren you tend with **too few hands**. Every visit it
names more needs than you can meet; you choose; the world visibly
changes; and what you left undone is remembered — kindly — and returns
tomorrow. Over days, one goblin at a time, it grows from a single
sleeping creature into a small society that acts by its own small rules
around the conditions you set. You never command it. You are the one who
returns. That — the operator's original wish — is the whole product:
*"the feeling of having a live little AI pet in front of me."*

## 3 · The five pillars — the only mechanics V2 recognizes

Everything after the crib is these five, **recombined at rising pressure
— never a sixth system.** Status is honest.

| # | Pillar | Law | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **Scarcity of care** | Always fewer actions than named needs; the unmet one is felt, not punished. | **BUILT** — Rung 4 is the first cell (2 actions, 3 needs, gates G4d–G4g). |
| **P2** | **Tomorrow remembers** | Every unmet need / deferred promise is a receipt that visibly shapes the next visit. | **HALF-BUILT** — `need3Deferred` is persisted; nothing consumes it yet. The promise is made and not yet kept. |

twelfth bespoke mechanic.
