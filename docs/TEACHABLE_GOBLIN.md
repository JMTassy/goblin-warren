# THE TEACHABLE GOBLIN — the inversion, built
<!-- answers docs critique "The Vending-Machine Fallacy" (HELEN OS notes, Jul 2026) -->

The critique was right: the answer→coin→unlock loop teaches the exact AI
misconception it means to dislodge (feed tokens, get goods), rations the
NPC magic as dessert, and can't falsify its own learning claim. This is
the inversion it prescribed, shipped as V0.

**Core verb: TEACH, not answer.** Every goblin card has a 🎓 TEACH panel.
The child types a fact in their own words ("light bounces off water").

**Interpretation is literal — hallucination made visible (R2 → curriculum).**
The goblin generalizes from what you *said*, not what you *meant*. A
keyword classifier (LESSON_THEMES) routes the lesson; comedic themes win
over generic ones, so "birds fly because they are light" → "MORE
FEATHERS. I glue them to this rock." The two hardest AI-literacy lessons
— models generalize literally, and confident output can be wrong — taught
with zero doctrine on screen.

**The seal, as a gesture (proposal ⊬ admission).** The goblin *proposes*;
nothing changes until the child taps STAMP IT. "nothing happens until
you stamp — that is the whole game." The HELEN constitution as muscle
memory, no vocabulary. (Gate-tested both ways: stamp admits exactly one
world object; not-stamping leaves the world byte-untouched.)

**Memory is the reward, not a coin (R3 solved structurally).** Each
sealed lesson is appended to `S.teaching.lessons` and becomes the
goblin's callback: the card's "remembers:" line reads "You taught me
'light bounces off water', so I made A Lantern." The magic is the core
loop, not its dessert.

**Coins demoted (R4 / R1).** Teaching costs and pays no ZOL (gate-tested);
capability is the channel — three lessons graduate a goblin (🎓). The
achiever's coin no longer taxes the explorer's curiosity.

**Falsifiability (R5).** The lessons ledger + callbacks are the
assessment instrument: what a child taught, what the goblin did with it,
and whether they corrected it, are all replayable receipts. The paper's
killing experiment (A: quiz-ZOL vs B: teachable goblin, delayed
explain-back) is now runnable because build B exists.

Gates: teach-gates.js 9/9. Baselines unbroken (verify 26 · lulu 12 ·
council 13 · levels 6 · echoes 5 · magic 8). The quiz loop survives as
an *option* (the 🦋 Riddle chip), not the spine.
