### [ledger] Fate-Replay Benchmarking: score surrogates on basin-of-attraction fidelity from genesis, not pointwise VRMSE
confidence: high

Read each trajectory as an append-only log whose long-term fate is a deterministic function of its genesis event (initial condition) under the solver-as-admission-gate. Then the right benchmark for neural surrogates is not short-horizon VRMSE but 'fate classification under replay': autoregressively roll the surrogate from the genesis snapshot and test whether the replayed log settles into the SAME attractor / absorbing state as the ground-truth log. The Well already contains the labels for free: viscoelastic_instability has four coexisting attractors (LAM/SAR/EIT/CAR) at one identical parameter set, distinguished only by initial conditions, plus edge-tracked trajectories sitting exactly on basin boundaries — a ready-made hard split. gray_scott_reaction_diffusion's card enumerates, by trajectory ID, which Gliders runs silently freeze into stationary states around t=121-159 — an absorbing-state label per trajectory. rayleigh_benard's card warns cell positions flip under 1e-3 IC noise, giving a third, continuous-fate variant (cell-count/position statistics rather than exact fields).

**Evidence:** polymathic-ai/viscoelastic_instability: identical equations and parameters (Re=1000, Wi=50) yield four coexistent attractors depending only on initial conditions; EIT vs CAR are visually near-identical and distinguishable only by a weak arrowhead structure; edge states (36/38/43/49 edge-transition trajectories) lie exactly on basin boundaries — natural adversarial test cases. · polymathic-ai/gray_scott_reaction_diffusion: the card publishes a per-trajectory 'confession list' of Gliders runs (e.g. training trajectories 81-159) that freeze into constant stationary states at known times — ground-truth absorbing-state events, i.e. ledger entries with timestamps. · polymathic-ai/rayleigh_benard: convection-cell positions are highly sensitive to 1e-3 initial noise and even the best surrogate (TFNO, VRMSE 0.6566) barely beats the mean — evidence that pointwise VRMSE is the wrong observable and statistical-fate metrics are needed.
**Novelty:** The Well's published benchmark (arXiv 2412.00568) reports one-step and short-rollout VRMSE only. Long-rollout stability has been studied (e.g. for weather emulators), but no published benchmark scores surrogates as classifiers of which attractor a genesis lands in, and none exploits edge-state trajectories as a purpose-built boundary test set — the viscoelastic edge-tracking data has, to my knowledge, never been used for ML evaluation at all. The frozen-Gliders list is treated by the card as a data-cleaning warning; inverting it into an evaluation label (predict the freeze event and its time) is new.
**Testable prediction:** Two surrogates with similar one-step VRMSE on viscoelastic_instability will diverge sharply on fate accuracy: specifically, basin classification accuracy on the edge-transition trajectories will collapse toward chance (~50%) while remaining high on deep-basin trajectories, and on Gray-Scott Gliders every tested surrogate will systematically mispredict freeze times (either freezing early or never freezing) rather than matching the card's enumerated t=121-159 window.
**Application:** A 'fate-replay' evaluation suite shipped as a thin harness over The Well (mirroring the Warren's replayEvents() discipline: same genesis, same gate, compare final ledger states). Directly reusable as the acceptance test for any surrogate intended to replace expensive solvers in multistable regimes — exactly where silent surrogate failure is most dangerous.

### [ledger] Receipt-Gated Rollouts: double-entry budget closure as an inference-time admission gate for surrogate steps
confidence: medium-high

Treat every source/sink term in the physics as a receipt that must be written to a budget ledger: in turbulent_radiative_layer_2D/3D the -E/t_cool cooling term and the net hot-to-cold mass flux are exact receipts (mass gained by the cold phase must equal flux through the cooling gate); in helmholtz_staircase the domain expert's stated evaluation rule is literally 'whatever the source injects must come out' (flux conservation). Product idea: wrap any autoregressive surrogate with a deterministic auditor that, at every rollout step, computes the integrated budgets implied by the predicted fields and ADMITS the step only if the double-entry books close within tolerance; denied steps are resampled or projected onto the closure manifold. Verification becomes bookkeeping audit — no ground-truth fields needed at inference time, which is precisely when you have none.

**Evidence:** turbulent_radiative_layer_2D: both phases start with heating exactly balancing cooling, and the entire dynamics is a net hot-to-cold mass flux driven by the -E/t_cool sink — a closed, auditable budget; FNO/TFNO score VRMSE ~0.50 (barely better than the mean) on it, so there is large headroom for a closure gate to catch drift. · helmholtz_staircase: the card states the expert acceptance criterion as flux conservation ('whatever the source injects must come out'), i.e. the physics community already evaluates this dataset by receipt closure, just not as an automated per-step gate; FNO's 0.00046 VRMSE here gives a calibration regime where closure should pass trivially. · polymathic-ai/supernova_explosion_64 and turbulence_gravity_cooling: energy and momentum are explicitly NOT conserved because of radiative cooling — the ledger view still works because the cooling losses are themselves the receipts; a naive conservation penalty would be wrong, but a source/sink-aware budget audit is exact.
**Novelty:** Physics-informed ML imposes conservation as a soft training loss or hard architectural constraint (equivariant/conservative nets). What is not published is closure as an inference-time acceptance test on off-the-shelf, already-trained surrogates — an admission gate in the Warren sense: the auditor never generates, it only admits or denies steps, exactly like admitProposal refusing non-ACCEPTABLE verdicts. It also cleanly handles non-conservative systems (cooling sinks) that break standard conservation penalties, because the sink term is booked as a receipt rather than treated as a violation.
**Testable prediction:** On turbulent_radiative_layer_2D, gating a pretrained CNextU-net's rollout on mass/energy budget closure (net phase-transfer flux consistent with the -E/t_cool receipts within a fixed tolerance) will measurably extend the stable rollout horizon — the timestep at which VRMSE crosses 0.5 shifts later — with zero retraining; and FNO's failure (VRMSE ~0.50) will be shown to correlate step-by-step with its budget-closure residual, making the residual a label-free early-warning signal.
**Application:** A drop-in 'ledger auditor' library for surrogate deployment (astro feedback emulators, engineering digital twins): every emulator step emits its budget receipts, an auditor replays the books, and only closing steps enter the trajectory log — giving operators a provenance trail of admitted vs. denied steps, i.e. a Midden Ledger for the emulator itself.
