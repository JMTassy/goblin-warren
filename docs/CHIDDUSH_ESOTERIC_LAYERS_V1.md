## Extraction (source → law → mechanic)

1. **Heindel, "Seven Days / Four Initiations"** → *correspondence is a
   lens, not a fact*: each weekday carries a planetary current and a color
   (Sun/indigo, Moon/blue, Mars/green, Mercury/yellow, Jupiter/orange,
   Venus/red, Saturn/violet; the whole week white). → The temple reading is
   tinted by the real weekday's current. Two paths in the diagram → the
   goblins walk the spiral; the Moth flies straight.
2. **Promethea (Tree of Life spread)** → *"the planets symbolize levels of
   awareness, as if they were actual places on that map… a place AND a
   state of mind"* → each Warren zone gets a symbolic register: Tree=memory
   · Garden=growth · Nursery=becoming · Spire=ambition · Forge=proof ·
   Gate=threshold. Readings connect events to zone-registers.
3. **Jerusalem** → *every event is preserved in storeys* → a reading has
   layers over ONE event: GROUND (what the log says, verbatim), GARDEN
   (zone-symbol + current), SKY (cosmic riff). Same event, stacked
   interpretations, none of them replacing the log line.
4. **Voice of the Fire** → *motifs recur across ages of one place* → the
   oracle may note recurrence (the mushroom returns, the mask returns) but
   ALWAYS renders it typed: "recurrence strengthens evidence; law remains
   unchanged" (WULMOJI_RENDER_LAW_V1, recurrence ↛ law).
5. **Moon & Serpent Theatre** → *the barker frame*: esoterica delivered as
   declared showmanship ("constructive lies") → every reading is hosted by
   a theatrical voice and footer-typed: "a reading, not a ruling — the
   Kernel did not stir." Poetry enters; the renderer checks its pockets.

## The core Chiddush

**Meaning is free; state is earned.** The temple is therefore the ONE
interactive thing in the Warren that pays no currency, moves no counter,
writes no replay entry. Symbols flow without limit exactly because they
cannot buy anything. NPCs may garden esoterism forever; admission stays a
hand elsewhere.

## Implemented slice (V1.4): THE LITTLE TEMPLE ⛩️

Permanent tappable shrine near the Mycelial Gate. Tap → a rotating host
(Cave Voice / the Moth / A Very Polite Mask / the Tree, half asleep) reads
the LAST logged event in three layers under the weekday current, with a
recurrence note when a sign repeats, footer always typed. Test-enforced:
reading changes nothing (replay length, orbs, sap all identical after).
