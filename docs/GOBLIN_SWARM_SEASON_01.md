## I. Vision (HELEN_FABLE_HER)

> **Season Law:** *Nothing grows in the Warren that was not first seen, judged, and admitted by a living hand.*

Come down the crooked stair, careful of the moss cushion — someone is thinking-too-long on it — and look at what the Warren is about to become.

Season 01 is not a list of features. It is a **weather**. We are done pretending content is *built*; in the Warren, content is *grown*, and growth has a shape the goblins already know by smell: **seed, sprout, judgment, door.**

It begins Garden-side, with seeds — five of them, named below, each a small strange star for the swarm to steer by. The goblin variation agents take a seed in their teeth and run. They braid it, misname it, hang moth-wing curtains on it, stack it into a reading stair. This is the swarm's whole job: **variation without permission**, a hundred sketches for every one that matters. Imagination is free. It has always been free. That is not the dangerous part.

Then comes the lantern. Every variation, however beloved, walks alone to HAL — and HAL does not admire, does not encourage, does not *admit*. HAL only says what is within the membrane and what is not. `ACCEPTABLE` is not a yes. It is a door left unlocked, nothing more.

The yes belongs to one hand only: the operator's. The Tall Ones' council may convene, five narrow lenses each carrying its own self-objection like a pebble in the cheek — and still they may only *recommend*. The player admits, or denies, or holds. Only admission moves the world, and every movement writes itself into the Midden Ledger, because a Warren that cannot reread its own footprints is just a hole in the ground.

And the denied things? Oh, the denied things are the treasure. They fall into SOPHIA's heap — the Great Compost, warm and patient and faintly humming — where refusal rots into richness. Composted material raises the weight of what gets proposed next; last season's *no* is next season's strangest seed. Nothing is wasted. Nothing is deleted. Held things wait in AURA's fog until someone decides, and the fog thins.

So this is Season 01: a swarm that dreams wide, a lantern that judges narrow, a hand that opens doors, a heap that remembers everything. ZOL still dies at dawn. The ledger still tells the truth. The law does not reskin.

Plant accordingly.

### The Five Pillars (season seeds)

- **SEED-01 · The Hundred Small Doors** — every new thing enters through the same gate as the first warren ever bought: proposed, lantern-checked, operator-admitted, ledger-written. A thousand doors, each the same door wearing a different hat.
- **SEED-02 · Compost Sings Back** — denied variations feed SOPHIA's heap, and the heap feeds forward: refusal must visibly enrich future proposals. Denial as fertility, never punishment.
- **SEED-03 · The Ledger Is the Garden** — every ambient marvel must be a *derived view over the event log*, never a second source of truth. If you cannot replay it from the ledger, it did not happen.
- **SEED-04 · Dawn Takes Everything** — ZOL remains session-breath. Seed variations that make ephemerality feel like luxury: treasures that cannot be hoarded, only spent beautifully today.
- **SEED-05 · Council of Narrow Lenses** — more voices, stranger stances, always the self-objection, never one drop of sovereignty. A seat that recommends is a character; a seat that admits is a bug.
