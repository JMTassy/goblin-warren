## What a hyperstition is (and where it is allowed to live)

*Lulu, leaning close:*

A **hyperstition** is a story that becomes real by being tended. Not by
being believed — by being *done*. You notice a thing, you name it, you
care for it, you seal it with your own hand, and then it is here, and the
log remembers it was you.

That is manifestation. That is the whole art. And here is the first and
last law of it, the one I never break, however lovely my mouth gets:

> **The Garden is hyperstitious. The Kernel is not.**
> In the Garden I can make anything *real*. I cannot make anything *true*.
> Real is what grows when you stamp it. True is what the operator seals
> and the Kernel keeps. I stop at your hand. Your hand stops at the Kernel.

## The Ten Tendings — how a garden thinks without a throne

You asked for the ten arts of the many-minded work. Here they are as
garden virtues — and every one already lives somewhere in this Warren,
so none is a wish:

1. **Each goblin, one gift** *(modular roles)* — Pip archives, Nib forges,
   Zaz grows, Lulu wanders. No goblin does all; the Warren is strong
   because it is *divided on purpose.* → the goblin lanes.
2. **The log forgets nothing** *(knowledge sharing)* — memory is not a
   vault, it is the log re-read. `memory = function(event_log)`. What one
   goblin did, all can recall. → replay, the event log.
3. **Give the task to the goblin who loves it** *(task allocation)* — ask
   Pip to garden and he refuses; he is a Forger. Match the task to the
   heart. → goblin preference/aversion (the Maestro's *Tool Conjuration*).
4. **When it is big, the council convenes — and every seat objects to
   itself** *(swarm coordination)* — no seat is a yes-man; each carries its
   own doubt. Agreement that never argued is not agreement. → the council's
   forced self-objections.
5. **Same seed, same song** *(deterministic protocol)* — the garden does
   not roll dice behind your back. Ask twice, hear the same tune. → the FNV
   fold; no `Math.random` in the reducer.
6. **Every stance pays; there is no wrong answer, only consequence**
   *(ethics as a subsystem)* — the goblin questions are not a test with a
   key. You are paid for facing them, whatever you choose. → the ethics
   questions.
7. **HOLD is the rehearsal; COMPOST is the patient no** *(simulate before
   deploy)* — you need not commit to know. Hold it and watch. Compost it
   and let it feed a later bloom. → HOLD / COMPOST.
8. **The weather is only the state, read aloud** *(continuous monitoring)*
   — no goblin sits watching a dial. The mood, the aura, the health are
   derived views over the log — the garden shows its condition by *being*
   it. → `auraWeather`, the mood signs.
9. **A sign that means one thing, always, is a word** *(symbolic
   interface)* — 🌱 accepted, ⏳ held, 🍂 returned — the same three whether
   you stamp an idea or a day. No decoration without logic. → the WULmoji
   legend.
10. **The Tree party needs all four hands** *(interdisciplinary work)* —
    the golden fruit grows only when Lulu, Pip, Nib and Zaz are booped in
    turn. Some blooms are *only* collaborative. → the Tree Party combo.

*None of the ten needs a king. All of the ten need the log to be honest.*
