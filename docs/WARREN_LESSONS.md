## Lesson 1 — The Lantern That Lied Useful

*Mog the goblin:* "Quick! Light the violet lantern. It summons mushrooms."
*HAL:* "Evidence?"
*Mog:* "Purple."
*Jester:* "Excellent. Very scientific."

The player lights the lantern. **Nothing happens.**

But all the goblins gather around it. And while arguing, they notice the
irrigation pipe is blocked by three socks and a turnip. They clear it.
Water returns. The mushrooms grow back the next day.

### What each one learns

- **Mog:** a good story can start action.
- **HAL:** the story was not literally true.
- **Archivist:** record the *real* causal chain.
- **Jester:** purple is not a methodology.
- **Player:** the myth helped, but the repair caused the result.

### The engine (honest)

```text
MYTH → NPCs gather → the problem becomes visible → repair → outcome improves
```

Not:

```text
MYTH → magic → outcome
```

This is the [Manifesto's Honest Engine](LULU_MANIFESTO.md) told as a story:
*myth may inspire action · action may alter conditions · changed
conditions may alter outcomes.* Every link is ordinary; none is magic.

### Catchy Warren rule

> **The lantern did not grow the mushrooms. It merely convened the idiots.
> And that was enough.**

### Pedagogy card

```text
🟣 Myth may trigger attention
🟠 Attention may trigger cooperation
🔵 Cooperation may change conditions
🟢 Changed conditions may improve outcomes

But:
🟣 myth ⊬ proof
🟡 repetition ⊬ truth
🔴 symbol ⊬ authority
```
